What the Smart Room is showing

This dashboard demonstrates how intelligent workspace devices make a room observable and actionable. Webex room signals and smart-room sensors produce presence, people-count, booking, acoustics and environmental context. Cisco Spaces converts that into occupancy context. Policy logic then decides whether to notify a user, release a booking, or send an automatic BMS control action.

How to read the twin

Colored sensors around the room emit packets. Packets land in Spaces for context, then route to Policy / AI and the correct action system: BMS, booking, or manual user control.

Notifications and explanations

Notifications are not just alerts. They explain why the system acted: which signal fired, what Spaces inferred, which policy matched, and whether the next action is BMS automation, booking release or manual comfort control.
